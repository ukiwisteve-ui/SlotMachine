About

Rift Riches is for players aged 18 and over. Coins have no cash value and can never be withdrawn or exchanged for prizes. Prototype 0.2, test mode: purchases are free.

Rules

Wins pay left to right on 20 fixed lines, starting from the first reel. Only the highest win on each line is paid. The Rift wild lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol except the Quantum Core.

Three or more Quantum Cores anywhere pay a multiple of your total bet and start a Rift Run: 10, 15 or 25 free spins where every win pays double. On each free spin The Collector may grab a reel and turn it fully wild, and wins through that reel pay triple. Cores during a run add more spins.

Six or more Plasma Orbs start Orb Lockdown. The orbs lock in place and you get 3 respins. Every new orb locks too and resets the respins to 3. When the respins run out, every orb pays its prize. Orbs can carry the Minor (25x total bet), Major (100x) or Grand (500x) jackpot, and filling all 15 spaces wins the Jackpot (2,000x).

Long-run return to player is 95.2%, measured over 10 million simulated spins.
